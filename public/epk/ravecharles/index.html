12matt3r, the brainchild of (sofa king sad boi) Rave Charles, is a fusion of creativity and a deep connection to various artistic realms—a clever play on “12” and “matter” that speaks to completeness, substance, and a holistic approach to design.

Rooted in Charlotte, NC’s underground rave scene, 12matt3r stands as a hybrid new media collective of digital, analog, and AI video artists who also DJ, rap, produce, and deliver genre‑defying live performances, drawing on metalcore, hip hop, and bass music without regard for strict genre lines.

Across 2014–2020, Rave Charles shared stages with artists like Griz, Tech N9ne, Juice WRLD, Hippie Sabotage, Wiz Khalifa, MGK, Lil Baby, G Jones and many more, touring with Cryptic Wisdom and Tripzy Leary, hitting festivals such as Digital Gardens, Breakaway, Camp Bisco, and Vans Warped Tour, and organizing Charlotte Basshead Meetup charity events—building a legacy of creativity, community, and nearly 400 shows across America.

Rave Charles · World Tour Snapshot

12matt3r grew from Charlotte’s rave underground into a multidisciplinary laboratory: digital, analog, and AI‑driven visuals, DJ sets, live vocals, and four‑deck mashups.

Across 2014–2020, Rave Charles joined tours with Cryptic Wisdom and Tripzy Leary, hit festivals like Digital Gardens, Breakaway, Camp Bisco, and Vans Warped Tour, and helped cultivate the Carolinas bass community through Bass Krew Collective and the Charlotte Basshead Meetups.

The timeline you’re exploring captures key dates from that run: basement takeovers, charity events, and festival stages that shaped the 12matt3r story.
