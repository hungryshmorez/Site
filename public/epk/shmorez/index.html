FROM CAMPFIRE TO CROWD

In a world where sugar meets sub-bass, SHMOREZ emerged from a freak campfire accident involving radioactive marshmallows and a malfunctioning bass cannon. The result? A sentient, molten creature with an insatiable appetite for heavy drops and crowd devastation.

Part marshmallow, part inferno, 100% bass chaos. SHMOREZ wields the power of filthy dubstep, gooey trap, and sticky acid bass — sounds so heavy they'll melt your speakers and your mind.
