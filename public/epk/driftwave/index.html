DriftWave Static is the sound of memories melting in reverse—a transmission caught between vaporized emotion and analog decay.

This beat tape is a trip down memory lane, featuring slushwave tracks that inspired me, reimagined and infused with my unique "slush sparkled" touch. It explores a sound submerged in fogged neon, where echo memory loops unravel across chrome beaches, and each beat dissolves under pastel rain. It's a fractured signal transmission—half-washed dreams and a splash of minimal dubstep pulses reverberating in sleep-mode.

The project aims to be not just heard, but felt, like tuning into a forgotten signal that only plays when you're alone and the world finally goes quiet. The 16 tracks drift between slushwave, instrumental hip hop, and minimalist fragments of dubstep—no drops, just slow tides. Inspired by the VHS static warmth of late-night vaporwave, the saturated reverb of geofenced memories, and the artificial calm of therapeutic detachment.

These tracks are "reinterpretations/(Reflections)," crafted as "love letters" to artists who have significantly influenced my work and the broader vaporwave scene.
