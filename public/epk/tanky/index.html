Biography

Tanky Johnson is the sound of a city suit burning on a gravel road.

Born from a sudden break with the corporate grind, Tanky’s music is the anthem for anyone who has ever traded a deadline for a lifeline and a view of the wide-open West.

A modern outlaw with a throwback soul, Tanky doesn’t just sing country; he chronicles the chaos of the American night. His sound is a dust-kicking blend of rowdy honky-tonk anthems and stark, neon-lit confessionals. He is the guy at the end of the bar who has seen it all—from the "Wrestlemania" hangovers to the quiet, painful wisdom found at the bottom of a glass in "Half Past Lonely."

Currently tearing up speakers with his Enter the Void project, Tanky is laying the groundwork for his highly anticipated debut album, "For Mama," slated for release in 2026.
