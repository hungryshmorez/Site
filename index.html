12matt3r
12matt3r is a genre-defying experience design collective where the digital, the analog, and the algorithmic collide. Comprised of video artists, rappers, and producers, the group dismantles the barriers between live performance and new media.
The name is a dual-layered philosophy: it bridges the concept of “12” as a symbol of cosmic completeness with the raw physical “matter” of our world—all while acknowledging the ultimate freedom found in the fact that, at the end of the day, a dozen or a zero, it simply doesn’t matter.
